Selected work

A few things I've built or studied over the years, described loosely.

  • Research

    Learned models for turbulence

    How well do machine-learned models of small-scale flow physics carry over to conditions they weren't trained on? Studied in two-dimensional decaying turbulence and checked against high-resolution simulation data.

  • Solvers

    Reacting flows

    Solvers for chemically reacting flow, written from first principles, from well-stirred reactors to propagating flames.

  • Computing

    Fluid simulation on graphics hardware

    Simulating decaying two-dimensional turbulence, and what it takes to make that run fast on modern hardware.

  • Ocean

    Tsunami early-warning support

    Tools that help early-warning staff turn ocean-model output into decisions.

  • Ocean

    Marine energy atlas

    A national map of India's ocean energy resources, built from satellite and model data.

  • Ocean

    Upwelling and fish landings

    Contributed to a multidecadal study of upwelling and fish catch along India's west coast.

  • Undergraduate thesis

    Heat transfer in two-phase flow

    A computational study of heat transfer in liquid-gas flows through complex geometries.
